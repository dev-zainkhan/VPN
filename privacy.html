Raaz VPN Privacy Policy

Effective 16 October 2026

Raaz VPN ("the app", "we") gives you free VPN minutes every day. This policy explains exactly what the app and our servers store, and what they do not.

What we never record

We do not record the websites you visit, the apps you use through the VPN, your DNS queries, the content of your traffic, or your IP address together with your VPN activity. Our servers keep no connection history.

Ads and tracking

The app shows no ads and contains no advertising or analytics tools. It does not use your advertising ID.

Sharing

We do not sell your data and do not share it with anyone for their own purposes. Our servers are rented from hosting providers (such as Amazon Web Services and Contabo), who process data only to run those servers.

Security

Traffic between your phone and our servers is encrypted with WireGuard. The VPN private key is created on your phone and stays in the phone's encrypted storage; it is never sent to us.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13.

Your choices and deleting your data

Uninstalling the app removes the device token and VPN key from your phone. To have the records on our servers deleted, email us with the device ID shown in the app under Settings, and we will delete them within 30 days.
